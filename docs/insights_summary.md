# Strategic Brief: Highway EV Charging Deployment — Chhattisgarh Corridor

**Target Audience:** Infrastructure Investment Committee / State Transport Planning Board  
**Objective:** Phase 1 fast-charging deployment recommendations to bridge inter-district transit gaps.

---

### 1. The Core Infrastructure Problem
While urban clusters (Raipur, Durg-Bhilai, Bilaspur) exhibit organic EV readiness, inter-city and freight transit along state corridors suffers from acute infrastructure deficits. Approximately **1,330 km of National Highway** operates without high-speed fast charging within a standard 50 km safe operating radius.

### 2. High-Impact Strategic Solution
Rather than capital-intensive greenfield development, the analysis identified **8 priority brownfield sites** (existing fuel retail infrastructure proximate to highway interchanges).

* **Capital Efficiency:** Utilizing operational petrol pumps minimizes land acquisition overhead, permits, and basic civil preparation.
* **Spatial Optimization:** A minimum 60 km separation between stations avoids self-competition while ensuring drivers never exceed 30–45 minutes of driving between charging opportunities.
* **Addressable Market:** The network encompasses **2.64 million citizens** within immediate 25 km catchment areas.

### 3. Phased Implementation Roadmap

| Phase | Target Corridors | Priority Hubs | Strategic Justification |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1 (Months 1–6)** | NH-53 & NH-130 (Raipur–Durg–Bilaspur) | 3 Hubs | Densest freight & commuter traffic; immediate commercial utilization. |
| **Phase 2 (Months 6–12)** | NH-30 North-South Corridor (Kanker to Jagdalpur) | 3 Hubs | Closes critical transit gap between northern industrial hubs and southern districts. |
| **Phase 3 (Months 12–18)** | Inter-state Frontier Corridors (Raigarh & Malkangiri border) | 2 Hubs | Connects state network with neighboring state transit corridors. |

### 4. Policy & Commercial Next Steps
1. **Grid Load Feasibility:** Partner with the State Electricity Distribution Company (CSPDCL) to verify 11kV/33kV substation capacity at the 8 identified geographic coordinates.
2. **Operator Partnership (PPP):** Form concession agreements with Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) already operating the selected candidate sites.
